Why blanket changes were avoided

Applying hundreds of indexes simultaneously creates severe write-lock and maintenance overhead. Revoking anonymous RPCs without reviewing intended public workflows (e.g., booking/seat availability) risks breaking legacy modules. Remediation must be targeted and evidence-based.
