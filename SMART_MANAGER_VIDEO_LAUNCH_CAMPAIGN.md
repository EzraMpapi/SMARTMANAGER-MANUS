# Smart Manager Video Launch Campaign and Email Outreach Playbook

## Executive intent

This campaign introduces **Smart Manager** as a Tanzanian-first business-management workspace for leaders who need a clearer view of sales, customers, inventory, finance, people, reports, and AI-assisted operational insight. The creative anchor is the 85-second Swahili promotional video. The campaign should lead with a practical promise—not exaggerated claims:

> **Dhibiti biashara yako kwa urahisi, kwa mfumo mmoja wenye nguvu.**

The recommended primary conversion is a qualified **workspace launch or demo conversation**, measured through tagged website visits, account-creation starts, completed workspaces, and sales-qualified replies. Do not promise guaranteed growth, market leadership, absolute security, or capabilities that are not available in the product.

## Video and channel plan

The 16:9 master is suitable for the website and YouTube-style placement. For vertical social distribution, create purpose-built 9:16 and 1:1 variants from the storyboard—not a crop of the master—so subtitles and key UI moments remain readable. The launch message must remain consistent across channels, while the first line adapts to local context.

| Channel | Asset | Copy direction | Publishing note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website landing page | 16:9 master video with poster frame and one CTA. | “Simamia biashara yako kwa mtazamo mmoja.” | Place above or directly after the hero; include a transcript and a direct `/app` link. |
| Facebook and Instagram | 9:16 30-second highlight plus 1:1 15-second teaser. | Lead with sales/stock/admin friction, then resolve to product clarity. | Use native captions; keep the CTA in the first two lines of post copy. |
| LinkedIn | 16:9 30-second operations/finance cut. | Speak to managers: reporting, visibility, accountable execution. | Link to a dedicated campaign URL rather than a generic home link. |
| WhatsApp Business | 15–30 second low-file-size cut, sent only to opted-in contacts. | One sentence plus a direct link. | Do not add recipients from personal contact lists without consent. |
| Email | Thumbnail/GIF or linked poster image that opens the master. | One pain point, one relevant product outcome, one CTA. | Use a marketing platform with consent, unsubscribe, and suppression handling. |

## Email outreach operating rules

Use a separate marketing-email platform or an already verified company sending domain. The Smart Manager product’s in-app transactional delivery is intentionally disabled, so this launch sequence must **not** be sent through the application Email Center. Send only to recipients who have opted in, are existing customers with a relevant relationship where outreach is permitted, or have explicitly requested information. Retain the source of consent, identify the sender clearly, include a working unsubscribe or preference link, and suppress unsubscribed, bounced, and complained contacts before every send.

Use the campaign parameters below consistently:

```text
utm_source=email
utm_medium=launch_sequence
utm_campaign=smart_manager_video_launch
utm_content={{email_number}}_{{segment}}
```

## Measurement framework

Track each stage separately so creative, channel, and product experience can be improved without guessing.

| Stage | Primary metric | Diagnostic metric | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Video reach | Qualified video plays | 25%, 50%, and 75% completion | If completion is low, shorten the opening and place the product reveal earlier. |
| Traffic | Tagged landing-page visits | CTA click-through by channel and audience | Shift distribution toward channels that generate qualified visits, not only views. |
| Activation | Account-start and workspace-completion rate | Step where users abandon onboarding | Improve the specific onboarding step before increasing ad spend. |
| Sales interest | Replies, booked conversations, or requested follow-up | Segment and email variant that generated each response | Prioritize follow-up by stated business need, not email opens alone. |
| Deliverability | Delivery, bounce, complaint, and unsubscribe rates | Consent source and send domain performance | Pause the affected segment if complaints rise; cleanse and honor preferences immediately. |
