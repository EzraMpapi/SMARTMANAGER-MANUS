# Smart Manager — Promotional Video Production Brief

## Production objective

Create a **75–85 second, 16:9 master commercial** for Smart Manager, a Tanzanian enterprise business-management platform. The master should use the official green, black, blue, and gold Smart Manager identity, restrained motion, professional Kiswahili cha Tanzania, and actual supported product capabilities. The final narrative moves from operational overload to confident control without inventing statistics, customers, security claims, or unavailable features.

> **Core message:** “Dhibiti biashara yako kwa urahisi, kwa mfumo mmoja wenye nguvu.”

## Verified product scope

The commercial may present the live dashboard, CRM and sales workflows, inventory, finance and reporting, people/HR, team and role-based operations, business analytics, and the built-in AI Assistant. It must not claim market leadership, guaranteed outcomes, absolute security, or any capability not present in the application.

## Visual direction

The opening environment is a plausible contemporary Tanzanian SME office or retail back office, photographed with warm natural daylight and a calm, premium technology finish. Product screens use the actual Smart Manager visual language: deep slate, emerald green, brushed gold accents, high-contrast clean typography, official logo, and subtle cursor-led interaction. Use clean compositing, slow purposeful camera movement, and transition by semantic relationship—clutter resolving into dashboard clarity, a sales KPI zoom into order/invoice flows, then inventory into reports through data visualization.

## Subtitle and sound specification

Use the voice-over verbatim as Swahili subtitles in a high-contrast lower-safe area; keep each line to two short lines where possible and never cover meaningful UI controls. The narrator should have a warm, confident Tanzanian Swahili delivery at moderate pace. Use low-volume premium corporate technology music that rises gently from the problem scene into the reveal, together with restrained UI tap, soft whoosh, and insight-tone effects. Music must remain beneath dialogue.

## Acceptance checks

The final master must have legible mobile and desktop subtitles, correct Smart Manager spelling, visible official logo, authentic UI colors, no fake metrics, no visual distortion, no unsupported claims, and a clear final call to action. Create separate 9:16 and 1:1 adaptations only after the 16:9 master is approved, rather than cropping critical UI or subtitles.
