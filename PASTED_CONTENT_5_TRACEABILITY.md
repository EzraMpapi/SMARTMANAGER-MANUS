## Requirements before analytical prediction or anomaly features

Before the dashboard can show a prediction, the product needs documented source selection, tenant-scoped training/evaluation data, feature definitions, model versioning, freshness metadata, quality thresholds, human review language, and a safe failure state. Before an anomaly can be actionable, it needs a durable baseline definition, a rule or validated model, false-positive handling, lifecycle audit events, and role-controlled escalation. The current readiness panel is intentionally a bridge to those future capabilities, not a proxy prediction system.
