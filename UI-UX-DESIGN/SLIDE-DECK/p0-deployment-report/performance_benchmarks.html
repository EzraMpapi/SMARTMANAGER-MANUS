Planner Execution Analysis

The new composite and actor indexes are successfully selected for exact predicates in representative plans where the planner expects selective access. The two NULL-heavy audit columns correctly remain sequential scans because all observed rows match the NULL predicate and a sequential scan is cheaper for tables with under 500 rows. The indexes are positioned to address future selective history lookups as data volume grows.
