Drain Procedure & Circuit Breakers

A staged rollout ensures that safe items are identified and processed without risking financial integrity. Hard stops automatically abort the run if anomalies are detected.

Stage 1

Dry-Run

Classify items only. No requeue or settlement actions are performed. Generates a bounded report.

Stage 2

Requeue-Only

Requeue safe items into the processing pipeline. Settlement remains disabled.

Stage 3

Test-Tenant

Settle safe items in a controlled test tenant to verify the end-to-end financial path.

Stage 4

Production

Small capped drain with two-person approval. Processes safe items through the service path.

Hard Stops Automatically abort the run and pause the provider account.
Any duplicate posting detected
Identity, amount, or currency mismatch
New conflict or provider status inconsistency
Lease churn or timeout/error threshold
Approval problem or cap reached
Unexpected provider state
