03 / Concurrency control

Advisory Locking & Bounded Leases

Safe concurrent workers require strict serialization at the account level and non-blocking row leases at the queue level to prevent race conditions.

Provider-Account Serialization Level 1

pg_advisory_xact_lock(...)
  • Single active drain run: Ensures only one remediation worker can operate on a specific provider account at any given time.
  • Transaction-scoped: The lock is automatically released when the transaction completes, preventing orphaned locks.
  • Namespace isolation: Locks are keyed by provider and merchant-account scope, allowing concurrent processing across different accounts.

Bounded Queue Leasing Level 2

FOR UPDATE OF p, r SKIP LOCKED
  • Non-blocking acquisition: Safely leases available rows without waiting for other transactions to release their locks.
  • Strict batch caps: Leases are capped at 10 items internally and 100 items per run to bound execution time and memory.
  • Lease expiration: Stale leases expire automatically and are reconciled rather than blindly replayed, ensuring fault tolerance.
