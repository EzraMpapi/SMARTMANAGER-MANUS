01 / Threat model

Why remediation exists

Provider callbacks are operational evidence, not permission to post money. The control plane must absorb retries while isolating signals that could indicate identity substitution or accounting risk.

01

Provider retries

Legitimate callbacks can arrive more than once or out of order.

02

Quarantine flood

Stalls, unknown states, and broken correlation create a growing backlog.

03

Identity conflict

A reused provider identity with a different fingerprint is never auto-settled.

04

Accounting objective

One provider event can produce at most one financial outcome.

Operating principle
Preserve evidence. Classify conservatively. Delegate settlement only through the idempotent service path.
The remediation worker is a controlled recovery mechanism—not a second payment engine and not a balance-repair tool.
