# SMART MANAGER ERP: Trilingual Customer Value and Subscription Summary

## Executive positioning

> **SMART MANAGER turns scattered business activity into connected, controlled, and explainable operations.**

The manual positions SMART MANAGER ERP as a practical business operating platform for Tanzania-ready organizations that want one authenticated workspace for customers, sales, stock, procurement, finance, people, support, property, industry workspaces, reporting, integrations, audit, and administration. The strongest promise is disciplined connection: capture work once, validate it at the server boundary, enforce it in the database, preserve evidence, and expose the result through the right management view.

## Subscription plans and tier limitations

The verified live commercial model contains **one free package and six paid packages**. Paid access is **monthly-only**. Each paid package provides **one paid calendar month plus one promotional bonus calendar month**, for **two total calendar months**. FREE_15 provides **15 days at TZS 0 with no payment**, and expiry transitions the workspace to a plan-required state without an automatic charge.

## Tier limitations to state clearly in a marketing pitch

The manual intentionally avoids presenting every listed surface as equally complete. **IMPLEMENTED** means a connected source, database object, or tested workflow is present. **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED** means the full end-to-end contract is not completely verified. **UI ONLY** means a surface is visible without a complete persistence claim. **CONFIGURATION REQUIRED** means environment variables, roles, storage, schedules, or provider setup are prerequisites. **EXTERNAL SERVICE REQUIRED** applies to TRA, AI, WhatsApp, email, mobile-money, or other provider-dependent paths. **PLANNED** or **NOT VERIFIED** is not a current feature guarantee.

For sales teams, the safe pitch is therefore: “SMART MANAGER gives your organization a connected operating foundation, and the platform expands with your verified business needs.” Avoid promising that every industry module, external integration, automated notification, or AI feature is available in every tenant without configuration.
