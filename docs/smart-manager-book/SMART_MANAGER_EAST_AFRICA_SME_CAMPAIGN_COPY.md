## Campaign positioning

SMART MANAGER ERP gives growing organizations one authenticated operating foundation for customers, sales, POS, inventory, procurement, finance, accounting, workforce, and selected industry workflows. The strongest entry message is practical: **record work once, keep control of the record, and make management decisions from visible evidence**.

FREE_15 is available at **TZS 0 for 15 days**, with no payment and no automatic charge. Paid packages are monthly-only and provide **one paid calendar month plus one bonus calendar month**, for two calendar months of access. The live prices are: TWIGA TZS 5,000; TEMBO TZS 10,000; SIMBA TZS 15,000; SIMBA SC TZS 4,500; YANGA SC TZS 9,000; and AZAM FC TZS 7,000. Use the exact prices in public communication and do not describe FREE_15 as a paid trial.

External payment, tax, SMS, e-mail, storage, and deployment services should be described as configuration-dependent where relevant. Do not promise automatic tax filing, guaranteed payment-provider availability, or full implementation of every industry workflow without confirming the customer’s configuration.

## Campaign safeguards

Keep the first call to action simple: **activate FREE_15, create one real record, and verify one useful result**. Do not lead with every module. Match the prospect’s operational pain to one workflow, then show how the connected foundation can expand.

Do not claim that a feature is fully live merely because a screen exists. Use the manual’s labels: implemented, partially implemented, UI-only, configuration-required, external-service-required, and planned/not-verified. Do not present the paid plans as annual products. Do not imply that a payment provider, tax filing integration, SMS gateway, e-mail service, or storage configuration is automatically available in every deployment.

## Measurement framework

Track campaign performance by segment and language. The most useful funnel events are ad click, landing-page visit, FREE_15 activation, first confirmed record, first dashboard/report view, first paid checkout, successful payment confirmation, package upgrade, support request, renewal, and churn. Compare outcomes by country, language, industry, package, and acquisition channel before changing prices or increasing spend.
