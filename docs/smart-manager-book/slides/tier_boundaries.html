SMART MANAGER ERP
Trust-led marketing playbook

Sell the value.
State the boundaries.

Trust grows when customers see what exists now, what needs setup, and what is roadmap-only.

“Start with the verified foundation, then expand with your real business needs.”

Don't present every module, integration or AI path as turnkey in every tenant. The status language protects customers.

IMPLEMENTED
A connected source, database object, or tested workflow exists.
“Available in the verified foundation.”
PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED
Partial delivery; end-to-end contract not fully verified.
“Review scope with our team.”
UI ONLY
A visible surface without complete persistence or operations.
“Interface present; workflow scope to confirm.”
CONFIGURATION REQUIRED
Roles, environment settings, storage, or provider setup are prerequisites.
“We configure this with your operating context.”
EXTERNAL SERVICE REQUIRED
An external provider (TRA, AI, SMS, payments) must be onboarded and connected.
“Subject to provider onboarding and credentials.”
PLANNED / NOT VERIFIED
A future or unconfirmed surface; not a current guaranteed feature.
“Roadmap conversation, not a promise.”
The sales advantage: customers buy clarity, not surprises.
VERIFY • CONFIGURE • EXPAND
