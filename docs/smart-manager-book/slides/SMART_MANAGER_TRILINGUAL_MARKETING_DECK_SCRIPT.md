# SMART MANAGER ERP
## Complete Trilingual Marketing Deck Script

**Languages:** English, Kiswahili, Français
**Audience:** East African SME leaders, operators, finance teams, partners, and implementation stakeholders
**Delivery style:** Confident, practical, evidence-led, and customer-focused
**Source boundary:** This script uses the published SMART MANAGER ERP manual, current repository evidence, and live subscription catalog values. It does not promise unconfigured third-party services or planned features.

## Slide 1 — SMART MANAGER ERP
### English
Welcome to SMART MANAGER ERP: your business, connected, controlled, and intelligent. Smart Manager is designed to give growing organizations one operating foundation for the work they already do—serving customers, selling, managing stock, recording finance, coordinating people, and expanding into industry-specific workflows. Today’s presentation is based on the official trilingual manual and focuses on practical customer value, the operating model, the module ecosystem, and the subscription path. Our promise is not more software for its own sake. Our promise is clearer work, stronger control, and better decisions from records the organization can trust.

### Kiswahili

## Slide 7 — Sell the value. State the boundaries.
### English
Customers buy clarity, not surprises. We use precise labels so the sales conversation stays honest. Implemented means the tested workflow is available in the verified foundation. Partially implemented means the end-to-end contract still needs review. UI-only means a visible surface does not yet prove persistence. Configuration required means roles, storage, company settings, or deployment variables must be supplied. External service required means a provider such as TRA, SMS, email, or payment infrastructure must be configured and authorized. Planned or not verified is a roadmap conversation, not a promise. This discipline protects customer trust, implementation quality, and long-term retention.

### Kiswahili
Wateja hununua uwazi, si mshangao. Tunatumia majina ya hali yaliyo wazi ili mazungumzo ya mauzo yabaki ya kweli. Imetekelezwa maana yake mtiririko umejaribiwa na upo kwenye msingi uliothibitishwa. Imetekelezwa kwa sehemu maana yake mkataba wa mwisho bado unahitaji mapitio. UI-only maana yake skrini inayoonekana si uthibitisho wa hifadhi ya data. Inahitaji usanidi maana yake majukumu, storage, mipangilio ya kampuni, au vigezo vya deployment vinahitajika. Inahitaji huduma ya nje maana yake mtoa huduma kama TRA, SMS, barua pepe, au malipo lazima asanidiwe na kuidhinishwa. Iliyopangwa au haijathibitishwa si ahadi; ni mazungumzo ya roadmap.

### Français
Les clients achètent de la clarté, pas des surprises. Nous utilisons des libellés précis pour que la conversation commerciale reste honnête. Implémenté signifie que le parcours testé est disponible dans la base vérifiée. Partiellement implémenté signifie que le contrat de bout en bout doit encore être revu. Interface seulement signifie qu’un écran visible ne prouve pas la persistance. Configuration requise signifie que les rôles, le stockage, les paramètres de l’entreprise ou les variables de déploiement doivent être fournis. Service externe requis signifie qu’un fournisseur tel que TRA, SMS, e-mail ou paiement doit être configuré et autorisé. Planifié ou non vérifié appartient à la feuille de route, pas à la promesse commerciale.

**Transition:** With clear boundaries, we can close with a practical invitation rather than an exaggerated promise.

## Accuracy reminders for the presenter

The paid plans are monthly-only. Each paid plan provides one paid calendar month plus one bonus calendar month, for two months of access. Do not describe FREE_15 as a paid trial, do not promise automatic charging, and do not state that every listed industry integration is fully configured for every customer. Present external payment, tax, SMS, e-mail, storage, and deployment services as configuration-dependent where applicable. Use the manual’s status labels whenever a customer asks whether a workflow is fully available.
