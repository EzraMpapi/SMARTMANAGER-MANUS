# Smart Manager Go-to-Market Playbook

## Product position

**Smart Manager helps growing Tanzanian and East African businesses run sales, invoices, expenses, inventory, people, and industry workflows from one connected workspace.**

Lead with one outcome: **from the first sale to a paid invoice, managers can see what happened and what needs attention next.** Avoid selling the product as a list of every module. The public homepage follows this position and routes visitors toward a live workspace, a walkthrough request, or a plan conversation.

## Primary customer segments

Start with one repeatable motion for growing businesses that need sales, invoicing, expenses, and inventory together. Use industry pages and demos as focused expansion motions for pharmacies, schools, clinics, and microfinance teams.

| Segment | Pain to lead with | Proof workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Growing businesses | Sales, stock, and expenses live in disconnected tools | Quotation/order → invoice → payment → executive view |
| Pharmacies | Expiry, purchasing, and sales visibility | Medicine catalog → batch/expiry → dispensing → sale |
| Schools | Fees, attendance, and administration are fragmented | Student → attendance/fees → report and follow-up |
| Clinics | Patient and operational records are hard to coordinate | Appointment → clinical record → billing |
| Microfinance | Collections and portfolio follow-up are manual | Borrower → schedule → repayment → PAR follow-up |

## Conversion funnel

1. **Awareness:** Publish a short workflow video or practical Tanzania business operations post.
2. **Landing page:** Send traffic to `/`, where the visitor sees the outcome, workflow, industries, and plan paths.
3. **Qualification:** Use the walkthrough/feedback CTA to capture the business type, team size, current tools, and desired module.
4. **Activation:** Guide the prospect into `/app`, create the workspace, add one customer/product, and create the first invoice.
5. **Value proof:** Review the selected-date Connected Workspace view and show the sales-to-payment path.
6. **Paid conversion:** Recommend Starter, Growth, or Industry based on activated modules and onboarding needs.
7. **Expansion:** Add inventory, people, finance, or a specialized industry module after the first successful workflow.

## Weekly content system

Publish three short videos each week using the real product:

- “Convert a sales order into an invoice without retyping it.”
- “Find unpaid invoices and follow up before they become overdue.”
- “See exactly what happened in your business on a selected date.”
- “How a pharmacy can track batch and expiry visibility.”
- “How a school can connect fees and attendance workflows.”

Repurpose each video into a WhatsApp status, LinkedIn post, Instagram/Facebook clip, and one practical article. Every post should have one CTA: **Book a walkthrough** or **Start a workspace**.

## Demo script

Keep demos to 15 minutes:

1. Ask what the prospect currently uses for sales, invoices, expenses, and stock.
2. Open the dashboard and select a specific date.
3. Create or open a quotation/sales order.
4. Convert it to an invoice and show the traceable source link.
5. Record or review payment status.
6. Show the executive view and the report/export path.
7. Recommend the smallest plan that solves the stated problem.

Do not promise integrations, savings, or regulatory outcomes that have not been verified for the prospect’s environment.

## Partner channel

Recruit accountants, IT consultants, business trainers, and payment/office-technology providers. Give each partner a demo account, one-page product sheet, a 15-minute demo script, and a tracked referral path. Pay commission only after a customer’s subscription or onboarding payment is confirmed.

## Metrics to review weekly

- Landing-page visitors
- Walkthrough requests
- Workspace signups
- First customer created
- First invoice created
- Seven-day returning workspaces
- Walkthrough-to-paid conversion
- Monthly recurring revenue
- Onboarding completion time
- Churn and top support reasons

The activation event to optimize first is: **a new workspace creates its first customer, product, and invoice.**
