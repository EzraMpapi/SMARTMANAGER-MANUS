## Caption policy

Each concept plate receives the lower-third **“Taswira ya maelezo ya mafunzo”**. Each redacted KMKM frame receives **“Skrini ya maelekezo — taarifa za rekodi zimeondolewa”**. The two labels must appear before the narrator describes the associated workflow.
