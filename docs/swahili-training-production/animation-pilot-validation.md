## Required review before use

An editor must visually confirm the acceptance requirements at normal playback speed, record any correction, and map the clip to a neutral opening or transition slot. The clip must not be assigned an approved narration or music mix unless that mix is separately reviewed. It must be removed from the master timeline if the review finds visible text, recognisable real-person likeness, private detail, or an implication that an abstract prop is a live SMART MANAGER workflow.
