**SMART MANAGER ERP**

**Msingi wa Kozi ya Kiswahili: Sura 01–06**

Kozi ya mafunzo ya ushahidi, faragha na uendeshaji salama.

## Slide 1

**Kutoka maono hadi kozi ya saa 8:30**

- Muundo ulioidhinishwa una sura 48 na dakika 510.
- Sura 01–06 hujenga msingi wa tatizo, mfumo, muktadha wa kampuni, usalama na dashboard.
- Master video bado haijarenderiwa; kila sura hupitia uzalishaji na QA kivyake.

## Slide 2

**Ushahidi unaongoza kila madai**

- Taswira za 3D zinaeleza dhana na hazithibitishi workflow, role, provider au transaction.
- UI hutumika tu baada ya ukaguzi wa faragha na ulinganifu wa claim-to-screen.
- Uwezo wa usanidi, huduma za nje, UI-only au partial huwekewa lebo ya ukweli.

## Slide 3

**Sura 01–02: Tatizo linaongoza mwanzo**

- Sura 01 inaweka maana ya ERP, mipaka ya ushahidi na ramani ya safari ya kujifunza.
- Sura 02 huanza na tatizo la biashara kabla ya kuchagua moduli.
- Wahusika wa mafunzo huleta mazingira ya Tanzania bila kuiga mtu au biashara halisi.

## Slide 4

**Sura 03–04: Muktadha kabla ya action**

- Safari ya dhana: mtumiaji → uthibitishaji → kampuni → jukumu → kazi.
- Utenganishaji wa kampuni unaelezwa kwa diagramu, si kwa schema au role assertion.
- Navigation iliyosafishwa hutumika kwa mwelekeo wa moduli pekee.

## Slide 5

**Sura 05: Usalama ni kanuni ya uzalishaji**

- Hakuna nenosiri, tokeni, session, email, profile halisi au siri ya provider kwenye media.
- Entry ya umma hutofautishwa na workspace binafsi.
- Profile, role na permission hutajwa kama dhana mpaka role-specific capture iidhinishwe.

## Slide 6

**Sura 06: Dashboard huanza na swali**

- Dashboard inafundishwa kama mahali pa maswali, signal, muktadha na hatua.
- Frame iliyosafishwa haina KPI, value, date, alert au tenant identity.
- Uamuzi unahitaji review ya binadamu; signal moja si hitimisho.

## Slide 7

**Capture ya KMKM ilihifadhiwa kwa redaction iliyohakikiwa**

- Dashboard, Finance, Inventory na Sales frames zilifunikwa header, identity, workspace content na badge/state zones.
- Tenant reference na Sales badge viliondolewa wakati wa ukaguzi wa kuona.
- Matokeo yameidhinishwa kwa module-shell orientation pekee, si evidence ya workflow au permission.

## Slide 8

**Msingi wa uzalishaji uko tayari kupanuka kwa udhibiti**

- Character bible, storyboard ya Sura 01, narration, theme na reusable conceptual visuals zimehakikiwa.
- Sura 02–06 zina scripts za muda, VTT cues, asset list, QA, Ulichojifunza na Kinachofuata.
- Chapters 07–09 zimepangwa kwa gates sawa: Sales frame salama; POS na CRM UI zinasubiri capture approval.

## Slide 9

**Njia ya master course ni chapter-by-chapter**

- Kila sura: script, asset boundary, UI review, VTT, audio mix na QA.
- Kila capture mpya: fully loaded, bila record, error, secret, credential, personal data au action isiyobadilika.
- Hatua inayofuata: Sales, POS na CRM zinaendelea kwa evidence gate; master assembly baada ya sura zote kukaguliwa.
