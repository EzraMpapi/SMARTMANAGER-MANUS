## Slide 3

**Sura 07: Mauzo yanahitaji mapitio**

- Mteja → hati → uthibitishaji → hatua inayofuata.
- Rasimu, mapitio na uthibitisho wa mwisho hutenganishwa kwa makusudi.
- Stock na fedha hutajwa kama muktadha unaoweza kuwepo pale inapowezeshwa.

## Slide 4

**Sura 08: POS inalinda utaratibu wa kaunta**

- Rejista ya mauzo, bidhaa/huduma, uthibitishaji na muamala ni vocabulary ya msingi.
- Marejesho, mkopo wa mteja na ulinganifu wa rekodi hutegemea sera na ruhusa.
- Checkout, payment, receipt na cash data havionyeshwi.

## Slide 5

**Sura 09: CRM huweka ufuatiliaji wazi**

- Lead, contact, opportunity, follow-up na pipeline hufafanuliwa kwa Kiswahili-first.
- Ownership na data quality huongoza hatua inayofuata.
- CRM UI na taarifa ya mteja zinasubiri capture approval.

## Slide 6

**Gates za UI huzuia overclaim**

- Sales: redacted orientation frame imeruhusiwa; sio transaction au role proof.
- POS: checkout UI haijapitishwa.
- CRM: contact, owner na pipeline UI haijapitishwa.
