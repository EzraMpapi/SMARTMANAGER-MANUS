## Shot / motion design

1. **Finance context:** blank cards and a soft connector line drift together, expressing context and review without currency, account, ledger line, or result.
2. **Accounting concept:** balanced geometric panels and empty sheets remain abstract; there is no journal posting, code, date, amount, debit/credit value, or closing state.
3. **Payroll privacy:** a conceptual shield and featureless fictional figures frame a blank-folder handoff; there is no employee identity, attendance, leave, salary, deduction, bank, tax, payslip, approval, payment, or payroll result.

The clip has no audio track. Narration, subtitles, and music remain separate editorial deliverables and must pass their own terminology, privacy, and claim review before any timeline assembly.
