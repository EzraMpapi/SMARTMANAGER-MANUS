# Sura ya 18 — Payroll na People Operations

**Muda wa mpango:** 14:00.
**Hadhira:** Timu za HR, wasimamizi wa payroll, viongozi wa idara, na wafanyakazi wanaohitaji kuelewa mipaka ya taarifa za watu.
**Ahadi ya kujifunza:** Mtazamaji ataweza kueleza kwa dhana employee lifecycle, attendance, leave, payroll preparation, calculation review, approval boundary na confidentiality bila kudai employee identity, attendance record, salary, allowance, deduction, bank detail, tax, payslip, approval, leave decision au payroll result ya mtu au kampuni yoyote.

> **Mpaka wa ushahidi:** Human Resources & Payroll imeorodheshwa kama **imejengwa**, ikiwa na employee, workforce authorization, Tanzania payroll, leave decisions na role approvals.[1] Hilo halithibitishi payroll run, payslip, statutory calculation, tax filing, payment, leave outcome, role assignment au employee record ya mazingira yoyote. Hakuna HR/Payroll UI capture iliyoidhinishwa katika pack hii.[2]

## Simulizi na mazungumzo ya Kiswahili

### 00:00–00:30 — Payroll ni faragha na uwajibikaji

> Payroll, au usimamizi wa mishahara, hugusa taarifa ambazo zinahitaji faragha na matumizi yenye kusudi. Sura hii haionyeshi mfanyakazi, salary, allowance, deduction, bank, tax, payslip, attendance, leave, approval au result halisi.

### 00:30–01:50 — People operations huanza na heshima ya data

> **Rehema:** “Kazi ya HR si kukusanya taarifa nyingi. Ni kutumia taarifa inayohitajika kwa kusudi linaloruhusiwa, kwa role sahihi, na kwa mapitio yanayofaa.”

> **Amina:** “Na payroll inahitaji mnyororo unaoeleweka ili hatua ya maandalizi isitafsiriwe vibaya kama malipo au uamuzi uliokamilika.”

### 01:50–03:15 — Mnyororo wa lifecycle

> Kwa dhana, lifecycle inaweza kuunganisha employee context, attendance au leave context, payroll preparation, calculation review na hatua inayofuata. Mchoro huu haionyeshi record, file, identity, shift, location, formula, payslip, approval au payment.

### 03:15–04:50 — Attendance ni data nyeti ya kazi

> Attendance ni taarifa ya kazi yenye mipaka ya matumizi. Haitumiwi kama taswira ya kuthibitisha uwepo wa mtu, mahali, muda, productivity au disciplinary outcome. Mfano wa mafunzo unasisitiza minimum necessary data, role, purpose na review.

### 04:50–06:30 — Leave ni mchakato, si ahadi

> Ombi la likizo linaweza kuhitaji maelezo, mapitio na uamuzi unaofuata sera. Hatusemi kwamba request imeidhinishwa, imekataliwa, imerekebishwa au imeathiri payroll. Hatuonyeshi sababu, tarehe, balance, approver, au record ya mtu yeyote.

### 06:30–08:25 — Maandalizi ya payroll na review

> Maandalizi ya payroll huweka taarifa zinazostahili kupitia review kulingana na sera, role na kipindi husika. Calculation review si payslip, si tax filing, si bank instruction, na si proof kwamba malipo yamefanyika. Hapa tunaonyesha boxes tupu za dhana, si values au formula.

### 08:25–10:00 — Ruhusa na usiri

> Mipaka ya role husaidia kulinda taarifa dhidi ya matumizi yasiyokusudiwa. Hata visual ya lock haiwezi kuthibitisha role assignment, RLS result, permission grant au security certification. Ni maelezo ya kanuni: kusudi sahihi, ufikiaji unaofaa, na ukaguzi wa matumizi.

### 10:00–11:45 — Mahusiano ya people operations

> HR, Payroll, Finance na Employee Portal zinaweza kuhusiana pale usanidi na jukumu vinaporuhusu. Mchoro wa dashed hauthibitishi posting, self-service action, bank file, payroll calculation, leave outcome au payment. Kila workflow inahitaji ushahidi wake uliopitiwa.

### 11:45–14:00 — Hitimisho

> **Ulichojifunza:** Payroll inahitaji faragha na kusudi sahihi; attendance na leave ni data ya kazi yenye mipaka; maandalizi na calculation review si malipo; role visual si permission proof; na HR/Payroll UI bado haijapitishwa kwa mafunzo haya.

> **Kinachofuata:** Sura inayofuata itaendelea na people operations na self-service kwa kutumia role-specific evidence tu baada ya capture approval, bila kuonyesha employee identity au record.
