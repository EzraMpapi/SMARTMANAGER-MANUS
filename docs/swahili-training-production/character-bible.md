# Character Bible: SMART MANAGER ERP Training

## Visual direction

The recurring cast is a consistent **professional Tanzanian corporate 3D/2.5D ensemble**, framed in a contemporary Dar es Salaam business setting. Every character uses a deep-emerald, warm-gold, navy, off-white, and charcoal visual system that supports SMART MANAGER branding without copying an individual’s identity or treating a generated figure as a real employee.

| Character | Role in course | Stable visual anchors | Voice and movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bwana Ezra** | Business owner / CEO; asks outcome-focused questions | Tanzanian man in his early forties, short neat hair, tailored charcoal suit, emerald tie accent, gold lapel pin, calm upright posture | Warm, strategic, speaks in practical business questions; restrained hand gestures |
| **Amina** | ERP consultant and primary guide | Tanzanian woman in her thirties, shoulder-length natural hair, deep-emerald blazer over ivory blouse, gold tablet folio | Clear, patient, authoritative; uses open-palm explanatory gestures |
| **David** | Finance manager | Tanzanian man in his thirties, navy suit, subtle gold pocket square, tablet showing abstract ledger lines | Measured, precise, never overstates figures; points to simplified diagrams |
| **Neema** | Sales manager | Tanzanian woman in her early thirties, warm-neutral professional attire with emerald badge, client notebook | Energetic but calm; follows customer journey and sales process |
| **Juma** | Inventory and operations manager | Tanzanian man in his mid-thirties, smart navy overshirt, emerald safety accent, barcode scanner/tablet prop | Practical, direct; demonstrates stock, warehouse, and receiving concepts |
| **Rehema** | HR manager | Tanzanian woman in her late thirties, refined charcoal suit with emerald scarf, employee folder | Friendly, privacy-conscious, explains policies and people processes |

## Continuity rules

The cast will not be used to impersonate a real customer, staff member, patient, or owner. Each scene carries the same face anchors, clothing palette, silhouette, and corporate animation treatment. Generated figures are labelled as **“wahusika wa mafunzo”** in the production metadata, not as product users or testimonial subjects.

## Dialogue roles

Every character asks or answers only within their domain. Amina can explain a platform concept; David can explain finance; Neema can explain CRM, Sales, and POS; Juma can explain stock and procurement; Rehema can explain HR and employee self-service. When dialogue changes to UI, the named screen element must be in a verified approved capture.
