# Sura ya 11 — Stock Control

**Muda wa mpango:** 12:00.
**Hadhira:** Wasimamizi wa ghala, wasimamizi wa biashara, na timu zinazopitia tofauti za stock.
**Ahadi ya kujifunza:** Mtazamaji ataweza kutofautisha count, record, tofauti, exception, adjustment na approval kama hatua za udhibiti bila kudai valuation, shrinkage, quantity, reconciliation, approval, au adjustment iliyofanyika kwenye tenant yoyote.

> **Dokezo la mpangilio:** Hii ni pack ya subject sequence iliyoombwa ya Stock Control. Haiondoi scope ya Procurement, Supply Chain au Manufacturing kutoka kwenye master course; topics hizo zitaendelea kuhitaji packs na evidence gates zao. Manufacturing inabaki na qualifier ya **imejengwa kwa sehemu**.[1]

> **Mpaka wa ushahidi:** Pack hii hutumia diagramu na Inventory orientation frame iliyosafishwa. Hakuna count sheet, adjustment form, value, variance, stocktake report, approval record, supplier data, au location detail iliyoidhinishwa kuonyeshwa.[2]

## Simulizi na mazungumzo ya Kiswahili

### 00:00–00:25 — Udhibiti si namba tu

> Stock Control ni utaratibu wa kuifanya rekodi iwe ya kuaminika kwa kuangalia muktadha, tofauti na hatua inayofuata. Hatuonyeshi idadi—*count*—valuation, loss, marekebisho ya rekodi—*adjustment*—au stocktake ya tenant katika sura hii.

### 00:25–01:40 — Count discipline

> **Juma:** “Idadi—*count*—inahitaji utulivu na ushahidi. Ukihesabu au kupokea taarifa ya idadi, usiibadilishe kuwa conclusion kabla ya kuielewa na kuipitia.”

> Hapa clipboard ni prop ya kubuniwa. Haina item, quantity, signature, date, area, au record ya biashara.

### 01:40–03:00 — Mzunguko wa udhibiti

> Tunaweza kueleza mzunguko kwa maneno matano: count, record, tofauti, review, na hatua. Count ni kile kilichoangaliwa kwa utaratibu. Record ni maelezo yanayohusishwa na kazi. Tofauti ni swali linalohitaji maelezo. Review ni mapitio. Hatua hufuata sera na jukumu linalofaa.

### 03:00–04:10 — Context ya Inventory

> Frame hii inaonyesha tu navigation shell ya Inventory. Haionyeshi quantity, stocktake, warehouse, role, trend, adjustment, report au user. Kwa hiyo, haitumiki kama uthibitisho wa stock-control workflow.

### 04:10–05:35 — Tofauti haimaanishi kosa moja kwa moja

> Tofauti kati ya observed count na record inaweza kuhitaji kufuatilia timing, movement, receiving, transfer, return, damage au record quality. Usitumie tofauti kutengeneza hitimisho la mtu, value au tukio bila evidence inayoruhusiwa na mapitio ya role husika.

### 05:35–07:00 — Exception review

> Exception ni signal ya kuangalia zaidi. Usifanye adjustment kwa sababu tu umeona tofauti. Tambua sababu inayowezekana, kusanya evidence inayoruhusiwa, fanya review, kisha fuata njia ya shirika. Diagramu hii haionyeshi adjustment form au approval record.

### 07:00–08:25 — Adjustment boundary

> Njia salama ya kufikiri ni: sababu, evidence, review, role, action. Kila neno lina kazi yake. Sura hii haithibitishi nani anaweza kuidhinisha, kuhariri, au ku-post adjustment. Role-specific proof inahitaji capture na session iliyoidhinishwa.

### 08:25–09:45 — Sababu zinazohitaji review

> Damage, return, transfer na receiving ni mifano ya mazingira yanayoweza kuathiri record. Si orodha ya sababu zote, wala si evidence kwamba matukio hayo yamefanyika. Kila mazingira yanahitaji record na sera yake.

### 09:45–10:55 — Scenario ya kubuniwa

> **Juma:** “Tumeona tofauti kwenye count ya mafunzo. Hatutadanganya record wala kutaja value. Tutaandika sababu ya kuangalia, tutatafuta muktadha, na tutampeleka kwa mtu mwenye jukumu la review.”

> **Amina:** “Hiyo ndiyo stock control: uaminifu wa mchakato kabla ya haraka ya kubadilisha namba.”

### 10:55–12:00 — Hitimisho

> **Ulichojifunza:** Count na record zinahitaji muktadha; tofauti ni signal ya review; adjustment inahitaji sababu, evidence, review na role; na Inventory frame haithibitishi count au approval.

> **Kinachofuata:** Sura ya 12 itaangalia Reports—jinsi ya kutengeneza swali, kuchagua muktadha wa ripoti, na kuepuka kugeuza chart au export kuwa claim bila source iliyopitiwa kwa faragha.
