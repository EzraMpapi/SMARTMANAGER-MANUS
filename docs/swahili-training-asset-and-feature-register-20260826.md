# SMART MANAGER Kiswahili Training: Asset and Feature Evidence Register

**Version:** 2026-08-26
**Use:** Pre-production control document for the long-form Kiswahili training course. It identifies what can be shown, how it may be framed, and what must never be inferred from a visual alone.

## Asset classes and permitted use

| Class | Inventory | Permitted training use | Not permitted |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Conceptual 3D visual | 10 plates, `V01`–`V10` | Chapter opening, abstract explanation, transition, high-level animated scene, and narrative framing | Proof of a live workflow, provider response, permission, transaction, or security certification |
| Authenticated UI evidence | 10 paired captures | Short, source-labelled evidence when the capture’s observed surface matches the script claim | Reuse as evidence for an unobserved nested screen, role-specific permission, or complete workflow |
| Approved brand assets | Smart Manager logo, owner portrait, 3 social banners | Opening/closing brand treatment and reference-aware promotional interludes | Reconstruction of private identity, credentials, claims, or customer context |
| Fresh disposable capture | Pending per chapter | Full UI demonstration after privacy review and verified safe demo state | Any private record, provider secret, personal data, or irreversible production mutation |

## Non-negotiable production constraints

> **No production secret, password, token, private email, financial credential, customer record, employee record, patient information, or unapproved screen capture may enter a chapter, subtitle, storyboard, or generated visual.**

The course will use an explicit truthfulness vocabulary: **imejengwa**, **imejengwa kwa sehemu**, **inahitaji usanidi**, **inahitaji huduma ya nje**, **UI tu**, or **haijathibitishwa**. It will never use a 3D concept or generic UI animation as proof that an external integration, real-time result, AI response, mobile app capability, or end-to-end workflow is live.
