# TRA VFD research used for the sales handoff

- https://www.tra.go.tz/page/efd-vfd-suppliers — TRA defines VFD as a cloud-based digital system approved by the Commissioner General to capture, transmit, and keep sales records electronically. It defines fiscal receipts as receipts/invoices issued using an approved fiscal device, government electronic payment gateway, or another approved electronic system. The page also states that users should use approved suppliers and report erroneous data or device failures through the approved path.
- https://www.tra.go.tz/page/know-about-e-fiscal-devices-efd — TRA describes EFP as an electronic fiscal printer used by computerized retail outlets and says fiscal receipts/invoices must be issued for sales where applicable. The page describes transmission to the TRA system and official supplier requirements.
- https://verify.tra.go.tz/ — Official TRA taxpayer receipt verification portal. It accepts a receipt verification code; verified receipts can be reviewed and printed on the portal.

Implementation boundary: Smart Manager opens the official TRA service and stores operator-provided TRA verification evidence. It does not claim direct fiscal submission or TRA acknowledgement without an approved provider adapter, credentials, certificates, and endpoint contract.
