# BusinessSphere ERP — Departmental Budget Thresholds & Alerts

## Overview
To prevent budget overruns, **BusinessSphere ERP (Smart Manager)** features persistent departmental budget thresholds (`departmentBudgets`) stored in user preferences and evaluated against live tenant expense records.

---

## 1. Default Departmental Budgets (TZS '000)
- **Operations**: TZS 25,000k
- **Sales**: TZS 15,000k
- **Finance**: TZS 10,000k
- **Warehouse**: TZS 20,000k
- **Admin**: TZS 30,000k

---

## 2. Visual Alert Levels
1. **Normal (< 80% Spent)**: Standard badge color with healthy utilization bar.
2. **Warning (80% – 100% Spent)**: Amber badge highlighting approach toward budget limit.
3. **Exceeded (> 100% Spent)**: Red alert badge surfacing budget overage for immediate executive review.
