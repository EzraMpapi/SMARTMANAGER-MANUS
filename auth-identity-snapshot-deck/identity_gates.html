03 / AUTHORIZATION LOGIC

Fail-closed identity gates

The ordered identity and tenant gates that must pass before the RPC reports authorized=true.

  1. Require auth.uid() (raises 42501 if missing)
  2. Require an active profile with company and role
  3. Require current_company_id() to match profile
  4. Require the company to exist
  5. Require a matching company_memberships row
  6. Require at least one workspace
Otherwise return authorized: false with a bounded reason.
